# QVision v0.10: Environments (orchestrator decisions)

User request (2026-11-20): follow Apple's custom-environment workflow (WWDC24 10087, "Create custom environments for your immersive apps in visionOS"). Reality Composer Pro is not available on Windows. Put the result in the Rendered area. First project: **Tradition Place, ground floor**. The reference renders in its `Randari` folder are the quality target.

## What Apple does (from the session transcript)
- Blender scene with lighting baked into textures.
- The scene is designed for a **viewing area**: "a circle on the ground". The viewer looks around and moves a little inside it. "It's not necessary to create geometry for areas that are not visible from the viewing area."
- UVs are packed into a few groups, each with one texture: their example has "6 UV maps and 6 textures" for one room.
- Rich materials, e.g. a floor built from a procedural layer, a photo layer and two variation overlays.
- **Reality Composer Pro only:**
  - imports the USD;
  - sets the materials to **unlit** and turns off "Apply Post Process" tone mapping;
  - makes glass a physically based material (roughness, opacity).

## Decision: our own replacement for Reality Composer Pro, at $0
Our bake scripts already write those unlit MaterialX materials (tone mapping off) into the USDZ automatically. The new part is the viewing-area approach. Each room becomes its own **Environment** package, and all the texture budget goes into what is visible from that room's viewing area.

## E1: Environment package (extends the Rendered package, R2 in v0.8)
The files are the same as a Rendered package (`.usdz` + `.json` + `.jpg`). The sidecar adds:

```json
{
  "kind": "environment",
  "group": "Tradition Place",
  "order": 1,
  "title": "Camera de familie",
  "viewingArea": { "center": [x, y, z], "radiusMeters": 1.5 },
  "start": { "position": [x, y, z], "yawDegrees": 0 },
  "hasSky": true
}
```

- `center` and `start` are at floor level, in USD Y-up metres. `yawDegrees` follows the same convention as before (rotation about +Y, 0 = facing −Z).
- File naming: `<Group>_<NN>_<Room>.usdz`, e.g. `Tradition_Place_01_Camera_de_familie.usdz`.

## E2: Bake (Blender, `tools/bake`)
- **Source**
  - `Tradition Place - Parter.blend` is read only; work in a copy.
  - Use the lighting, world, sun and camera setup **already in that blend**: it produced the reference renders, so the bake should match them.
- **Environments**
  - One per reference render / scene camera: family room, living, dining, kitchen, ground-floor bedroom, facade and portico.
  - Viewing-area centre: the camera's floor position (or the room centre if the camera sits outside the room); radius about 1.5 m.
- **Visibility**
  - Sample about 50–100 eye points inside the viewing area at 1.2–1.8 m height.
  - Faces never visible from them are dropped. Faces seen only at grazing or far distances get low density.
- **Texel density from the viewer**
  - Target about one texel per display pixel: Vision Pro ≈ 34 px/degree, i.e. about 0.5 mm per metre of distance.
  - Close surfaces (≤ 2 m) get about 1 mm per texel; far ones proportionally less.
  - Budget per environment: ≤ 12 × 4096² atlases (about 1 GB in memory) and ≤ ~800 k triangles. Only one environment is loaded at a time.
- **Bake content**
  - Final view-independent colour (diffuse + transmission + emission), as v1, at the new density.
  - **Reflections, if feasible:** glossy reflections seen from the viewing-area centre on floors, glass, metals and lacquer. Research the best Cycles approach, e.g. bake glossy with the view direction fixed to the centre point, or render a cubemap from the centre and project it. If it is too risky, ship without reflections and say so.
- **Far field**
  - Everything beyond the house (garden, trees, sky, neighbours) is rendered as a **360° equirectangular panorama** from the viewing-area centre, at about 8K, and mapped onto a large inside-facing sphere or dome in the USDZ, unlit. Then the view through the windows matches the renders, including the trees, at almost no cost.
  - Near exterior elements (terrace, the garden ground near the glazing) stay as geometry.
- **Tone mapping:** the same view transform, look and exposure as the blend's own render settings. Match the reference renders.
- **Glass:** see-through tint as v1; if reflections are baked, a faint reflection layer.
- **Config:** `tools/bake/tradition_place.json`, **git-ignored** (it holds client paths); a generic example goes into `example_config.json`.
- **Verification**
  - Previews from the viewing-area centre at each reference camera's angle, unlit, the way the headset shows them.
  - A side-by-side sheet with the reference renders per room: `previews_env/<room>_vs_ref.jpg`.
  - Fix black spots, seams, missing textures and noise before shipping.

## E3: App (Rendered tab)
- **Environments section** above "On this Vision Pro" / "On my PC", grouped by `group`, cards in `order`. Download one, or the whole group.
- **Viewer behaviour** for `kind: environment`:
  - Full immersion; the user stands at `start` (inside the viewing area), facing `yawDegrees`.
  - **No fist-fly.** You look around and walk physically.
  - Leaving the viewing area by more than about 1 m slowly fades the scene with a hint: "Step back to the centre".
  - The palm menu shows the **other rooms in the group** (instead of Viewpoints). Choosing one: fade out, unload the current one, load the next, fade in.
  - Exit as always.
- Everything else (download, update, resume, Continue, Settings) works as in v0.9.
- **Version:** 0.10.0 (build 12).
