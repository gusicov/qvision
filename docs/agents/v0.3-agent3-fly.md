## User feedback
"Fly mode should be a bit more sensitive the farther you move from the point where you clenched your fists, so you can go faster."

## Changes
1. **Translation speed curve.** `x = max(0, |d| − 0.025)` m, where d is the fist-midpoint offset from the grab point.
   - `speed(x) = (2.5·x + 45·x²) · speedMultiplier` in m/s.
   - That gives about 0.26 m/s at 5 cm, 0.9 at 10 cm, 2.3 at 20 cm, 4.8 at 30 cm and 8.2 at 40 cm.
   - Cap: `14 · speedMultiplier` m/s.
   - Direction = d normalised (unchanged).
2. **Responsiveness.** Speed-up damping 8 → 10/s (`1 − exp(−dt·10)`). Slow-down when the fists open: 6/s, so stopping stays smooth.
3. **Yaw curve.** Keep the 5° deadzone. Then `ω = (1.2·a + 2.0·a²)` rad/s, where a is the excess angle in radians, capped at 1.6 rad/s.
4. **Indicator feedback.** Scale the arrow length with the current speed (0.03 m at rest to 0.12 m at ≥ 5 m/s), and shift the ring tint from cyan toward white as speed rises. Keep it cheap: update the material only when the speed bucket changes, in 4 buckets.
5. **Hand panel support** in HandTrackingService. Make `palmFacingUser` less flickery: enter > 0.55, exit < 0.35, plus a 0.12 s minimum hold. Keep the public API unchanged.
